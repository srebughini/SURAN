Privacy Policy

Last updated: to be defined before launch.

Data controller

SURAN is, at this stage, an independent project created to validate an idea — it is not yet a registered company. The data collected through this site is handled by Stefano Rebughini, who can be reached at privacy@suran.tech for any question about this policy or your data.

What data we collect

We only collect the data you voluntarily provide when applying to the Beta Program through the form on the home page: name, email address, smartphone model and, if you choose to share them, optional details about how you use your phone and your motivations.

We don't install profiling cookies. To understand how many people visit the site, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a tool that doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect data that could identify you — it only measures visit counts in aggregate, anonymous form.

How we use your data

We use your data exclusively to evaluate your Beta Program application and to contact you about it. We don't sell it, don't share it with third parties for marketing purposes, and don't use it for anything other than what you provided it for.

Legal basis and retention

We process your data based on the consent you give by submitting the form. We keep it for as long as needed to manage the Beta Program, and in any case no longer than 24 months from when you applied, unless you request earlier deletion.

Third-party services

The form on the home page sends data through Formspree, the service we use to receive applications by email. Formspree acts as a data processor on our behalf; you can read their privacy policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, or receive it in a portable format, by writing to ste.rebu@outlook.it. If you believe the processing of your data violates applicable law, you have the right to file a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy over time, for example if we change the tools we use to collect applications. The most up-to-date version will always be available at this address.
